Protect your future with life insurance and legacy protection strategies. Learn about life insurance benefits, how to manage estate planning costs, and secure your family's financial stability.

### Do I actually need life insurance if I already have some at work?

Most families rely on employer coverage without realizing it usually disappears when the job does.

### How much life insurance do I really need for my family?

People either guess or buy the cheapest option without understanding income replacement, debt, and future costs. A financial review and your needs will determine that.

### Is life insurance only for death, or can it be used while I am alive?

Very few people understand living benefits and access options. Living benefits is a feature built into life insurance you can use while you are still alive. Check with the carrier on requirements and offering.

### What happens to my family financially if something happens to me?

Ask yourself this: how could my family continue living the lifestyle I have provided if something happens to me?

### How do I protect my home if I pass away?

Mortgage protection is one of the biggest family concerns. Booking an appointment will give clarity on how you can protect your home.

### What is the difference between term and permanent life insurance?

Think of Term as Temporary and Permanent as Lifetime.

### How do I make sure my children are taken care of financially?

An estate plan and a customized strategy is a great way to ensure both financial and home stability is achieved.

### Do I need a will or a trust, and what is the difference?

A Will and Testament outlines how you want your property and assets distributed after your death. A trust is when you transfer property ownership to a trustee. It is common for many people to have both.

### How do I protect my income if I get sick or injured?

Disability is more common than death before retirement. Should any of the qualifying triggers of Chronic, Critical or Terminal illness become a diagnosis, Living Benefits can give you up to 90% of your face amount to cover income gaps and/or medical bills.
